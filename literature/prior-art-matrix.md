# Prior-art route matrix

This matrix records scope, not a verdict on the value of a result. It is provisional until full-text theorem audits are logged.

| Result family | Dimension | Regulator/volume | Gauge scope | Rigorous asset | Missing for Clay target |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|
| Wilson (1974) | 4 | finite lattice | compact lattice groups | exact gauge-invariant regulator | cutoff removal, continuum axioms, nontriviality, finite physical gap |
| Osterwalder–Seiler (1978) | lattice | fixed spacing; strong-coupling analysis | lattice gauge theory | reflection positivity and constructive strong-coupling structure | weak-bare-coupling continuum trajectory and full limit |
| Lüscher (1977) | lattice | fixed spacing/finite volume framework | Wilson theories | positive transfer matrix | regulator-uniform physical spectral gap |
| Bałaban RG series | 4 | ultraviolet block analysis | non-Abelian lattice gauge fields | localized effective actions, coupling flow, cluster/large-field control | local composite Schwinger functions, IR bridge, OS continuum theory, gap |

| Strong-coupling functional inequalities | lattice | strong bare coupling | selected \(SU(N)\) settings | mixing/Poincaré/log-Sobolev bounds | connect weak bare coupling to massive basin; OS Hamiltonian identification |
| Stochastic gauge constructions | mainly 3 | distributional/local dynamics | selected groups and matter models | rigorous singular gauge-orbit/state-space tools | critical 4D pure theory, invariant OS-positive measure, gap |
| Large-\(N\) expansions | lattice/limit | strong coupling and/or \(N\to\infty\) | primarily \(SU(N)\) limit | rigorous loop/string expansions in stated regimes | fixed \(N\), arbitrary compact simple groups, continuum limit interchange |

## Mandatory comparison fields for a candidate lemma

- exact theorem and page;
- dimension and geometry;
- gauge group and matter content;
- bare-coupling regime;
- small- and large-field coverage;
- volume and cutoff dependence of constants;
- observables controlled and their renormalization;
- positivity/axiom consequences;
- novelty delta, if any.
