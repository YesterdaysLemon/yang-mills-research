# Full-problem audit checklist

`Open` is not a failing grade for an exploratory repository; it is a prohibition on solution claims.

| Gate | State | Evidence | Blocking question |
|---|---|---|---|
| Exact official statement | Partial | `PROBLEM.md` | Obtain external scope review |
| Every compact simple group | Open | — | Is there a valid reduction or group-uniform construction? |
| UV regulator removal | Open | — | Which topology and renormalized observables converge? |
| Infinite-volume limit | Open | — | Which bounds are volume-uniform? |
| Nontriviality | Open | — | Which limiting connected observable is provably non-Gaussian/nonzero? |
| Local gauge-invariant fields | Open | — | How are composite fields defined and renormalized? |
| Required short-distance behavior | Open | — | Where is asymptotic freedom matched rigorously? |
| OS/Wightman-strength axioms | Open | — | Are all reconstruction hypotheses verified? |
| Unique vacuum and positive energy | Open | — | What construction supplies the spectral framework? |
| Finite positive mass gap | Open | — | Which physical-unit bound survives both limits? |
| Self-contained manuscript | Open | — | No manuscript exists |
| Independent expert review | Open | — | No reviewer is recorded |
| Qualifying publication/process | Open | — | Premature until a complete proof exists |

## Line-by-line audit prompts

- Are all quantifiers and constant dependencies explicit?
- Is every cited theorem's exact hypothesis checked?
- Are domains of unbounded operators and distributional operations defined?
- Are interchanges of limits, integrals, sums, expectations, and derivatives justified?
- Is any compactness argument followed by identification of the limit?
- Is a regulator-scale statement being mistaken for a physical-scale statement?
- Is the reasoning circular between clustering, uniqueness, and the gap?
- Can a nearby free, abelian, lower-dimensional, finite-volume, or topological model satisfy the written hypotheses and falsify the intended conclusion?
